flurry flur-i ◆◆◆◇◇

US ˈflʌri · ˈflɜːri

1 noun [N-COUNT]

A flurry of activity, or a brief spell of snow.

  • There was a flurry of snow overnight.
  • A flurry of excitement spread through the crowd.
    A sudden wave of excitement spread through the crowd.

Synonyms gust

Antonyms lull

2 verb [V]

informal To move about nervously or in a hurried manner.

  • She flurried around the kitchen before the guests arrived.

3

See flurry for the noun sense.

See also blizzard

Derived terms flurry up

Forms flurries (plural), flurried (past), flurrying (present participle)
