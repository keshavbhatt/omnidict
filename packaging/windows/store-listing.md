## Description (up to 10000 characters)

Omnidict is a fast, free offline dictionary and thesaurus for Windows. Download the dictionaries you need, once, and look up word meanings, definitions, synonyms, translations and pronunciation instantly, anywhere, without an internet connection.

More than 600 dictionaries in over 300 languages are free to download: a complete English dictionary, an English thesaurus, dictionaries for Spanish, French, German, Hindi, Arabic, Japanese, Chinese, Korean and hundreds more languages, and bilingual dictionaries for translating words between English and 30 other languages.

Read a word you do not know in your browser, a PDF or a document? Select it and press Ctrl+Alt+D: its meaning opens in a small window, without leaving what you are doing.

FREE TO START
Omnidict is free to download, with a 14-day trial of every feature. After the trial it stays free with one dictionary searched at a time. Omnidict Pro, an optional upgrade, searches all your dictionaries at once and adds quick lookup from any app.

ENGLISH DICTIONARY AND THESAURUS
• Word meanings and definitions with example sentences, word forms and pronunciation (IPA)
• English thesaurus: synonyms, antonyms and related words for every sense of a word
• Ideal for students, English learners (ESL), writers, translators and word game players

A DICTIONARY FOR EVERY LANGUAGE
• Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Hindi, Arabic, Turkish, Japanese, Chinese, Korean and over 300 more languages, each explained in English
• Bilingual translation dictionaries: English to Spanish, English to French, English to German, English to Hindi, English to Arabic, English to Japanese and more, plus hundreds of other language pairs such as German-English and Spanish-Portuguese
• Japanese-English, Chinese-English (Mandarin, with pinyin) and Korean-English dictionaries
• Latin, Ancient Greek, Sanskrit, Old English and Old Norse for students of classical and historical languages
• Filter the catalogue by language, and see each dictionary's size and number of words before you download it

FAST, SMART WORD SEARCH
• Results as you type, with the best match first
• Finds the base word from its other forms: "ran" finds "run", "mice" finds "mouse"
• Accents are optional: type "cafe" to find "café"
• "Did you mean" spelling suggestions when you are not sure how a word is spelled
• Wildcard search for crosswords, word games and puzzles: ? for one letter, * for any number of letters
• Reverse dictionary: full text search finds a word from words in its definition
• Click any word in a definition to look it up, with Back and Forward as in a browser

QUICK LOOKUP FROM ANY APP (PRO)
• Select or copy a word anywhere and press Ctrl+Alt+D, or a shortcut you choose
• Reads the selected word in Edge, Chrome, Word, Notepad and most Windows apps; anywhere else, copy the word first
• Waits in the notification area so quick lookup is always ready, and can start with Windows

BUILD YOUR VOCABULARY
• Favourites and recent lookups to learn and review new words
• Turn dictionaries on or off, reorder them, update or remove them
• Light and dark themes that follow Windows, and adjustable text size
• Keyboard shortcuts for everything

WORKS OFFLINE, STAYS PRIVATE
• Once a dictionary is downloaded, every lookup works offline: on a plane, at school, at work or anywhere without internet
• Private: no ads, no tracking, no sign-in; your lookups never leave your computer
• Small and fast: dictionaries are compact and open instantly

Dictionary content comes from Wiktionary, WordNet, FreeDict, JMdict, CC-CEDICT and Kengdic; the About window credits each source.
