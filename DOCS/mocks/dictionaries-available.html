Notes

  1. Two at a time.German - English and Japanese - English download together; Chinese - English (CC-CEDICT) waits its turn and starts as soon as a slot frees up.
  2. Keeps going.Closing this sheet does not pause downloads. The header's Dictionaries button then shows a small progress ring so their status is still visible.
  3. Local catalogue.The list here comes from a catalogue served on localhost:8000 for development; nothing is published anywhere until the owner decides to.
  4. Refresh (approved 2026-09-28).The catalogue is read when this sheet opens if the copy on disk is more than 24 hours old, and on "Check for updates". Refresh (or F5) reads it now. It is also read again on its own when a download finds the server's file newer than the catalogue ("This dictionary was updated on the server; try again"), so Retry gets the new file.
  5. On failure.A download that fails shows Retry in place of its progress, with a short reason such as "Connection lost".
  6. Catalogue count (approved 2026-09-28).The tab says how many dictionaries the catalogue offers, "Available (308)"; when a filter narrows the list, the footer says "Showing 24 of 308 dictionaries".
  7. Provider filter (approved 2026-09-28).A third drop-down next to From and To: Any, Wiktionary, FreeDict, WordNet, JMdict, CC-CEDICT, Kengdic, each with its count ("Wiktionary (14)"). It filters on the catalogue's source, so a new user can pick "Wiktionary" and find English straight away. All three drop-downs open a short scrolling list (12 rows), never a list the height of the screen.
  8. Entries column (approved 2026-09-28).Each row shows its number of entries, right-aligned in its own column before the size, so dictionaries can be compared at a glance. The number comes from the catalogue (`entry_count`), no schema change. The column stays while a row downloads.
